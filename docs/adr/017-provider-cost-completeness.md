# Provider contribution requires confirmed full costs

Status: Accepted guard for unresolved stakeholder inputs

Decision: Calculate observed attributed revenue and expense directly. Compute fully loaded cost and contribution only when an explicit provider/date-range/basis declaration confirms that the attributed expense records are complete for that range. Do not infer benefits, malpractice, overhead allocation or compensation from role/name.

Consequences: F2.5 works when its required data is available; otherwise its unavailable state is explicit. Section 12 questions 3, 4 and 6 remain unresolved for real clinic data. No absent expense is silently described as a complete provider cost.
